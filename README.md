# Suleiman Ibrahim Inuwa

Agricultural economist and software developer based in Kano, Nigeria. I use data,
machine learning and software to make rural and agricultural welfare easier to
measure and manage.

## What I work on

**Satellite-based wealth prediction.** An open, reproducible pipeline that predicts
household wealth from free satellite data and Demographic and Health Surveys across
five African countries, with a focus on whether accuracy holds inside rural and urban
areas and in new countries.
→ [nigeria-poverty-mapping](https://github.com/suibing15/nigeria-poverty-mapping)

**Educational software.** I developed SUIBING, a school management platform with
automated report generation, online and offline computer-based exams, parental access
control, ID-card generation and enrolment analytics. It has been used at
Assalam International Academic School, Garko, and Ubayyu binkaab Secondary school Garko

**Agricultural economics research.** Time-series and productivity analysis of wheat in
Nigeria, India and Pakistan (ARIMA, ARDL, Törnqvist-Theil index), and forecasting of
cashew exports.
